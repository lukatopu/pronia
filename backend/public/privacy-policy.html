Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

This policy describes how the Pronia demonstration plant-store project at pronia.onrender.com handles information. For questions or requests about your information, contact the project operator at proniawebsite@gmail.com.

Information you provide

When you register, we store your first name, last name, email address and a password hash. We also store cart items, wishlist items, saved addresses and order summaries associated with your account. Addresses may include your name, company, street, city, state, postcode, country, email and phone number.

This is a demonstration project. The current checkout records an order summary; it does not collect card details or process card payments. The current contact form displays a demonstration confirmation without submitting its contents to the backend.

How information is used

Information is used to create and authenticate accounts, display saved shopping information, update profiles, record demonstration orders and send requested password-reset emails. Reset links expire after 15 minutes. Email acceptance by a sending provider does not guarantee delivery to your inbox.

Storage, hosting and email providers

Account and shopping data are stored in MongoDB Atlas. Render hosts the website and backend. Hosting and infrastructure providers may process connection information, such as IP addresses and request logs, to operate their services.

Depending on the deployed email configuration, password-reset messages are sent through Resend or the project operator's Gmail account using Google services. The sending provider processes the recipient address and message, including the reset link. The project does not sell account information.

Google account access

If the Gmail API integration is enabled, the project operator authorizes the Pronia sender account with Google's gmail.send permission solely to send project emails, including requested password resets. This integration does not request permission to read Gmail inboxes. Visitors do not authorize access to their Google accounts to receive email. Sender authorization credentials are intended for server-side configuration and are not used for advertising.

Cookies and browser storage

A login cookie authenticates your session and is configured to expire after one day. Browser local storage holds language and currency preferences and the product selected for comparison. You can clear these through your browser settings; clearing the login cookie signs you out.

Other services

The contact page embeds Google Maps, and the website loads fonts from Google Fonts. Requests to these services may share connection information with Google under its privacy policy. External links lead to services with their own policies.

Retention and your choices

The project currently has no automatic account-data deletion schedule. Account and shopping information remain in the project database until removed by the operator or the database is reset. You can update profile information and remove cart and wishlist items through the website. Contact proniawebsite@gmail.com to request access, correction or deletion of account information; the operator may need to verify that you control the account.

The sender-account owner can revoke the project's Google authorization through their Google account settings. Revocation stops Gmail sending until the sender is authorized again.

Policy updates

This page may be updated when the project's features or providers change. The date above identifies the latest revision.
